# Document Templates Content Guide

This guide details the recommended content, layout, and purpose for each of the official document templates in the Risabu Connect ERP.

---

## 1. Admission Letter (`official_admission_letter`)
* **Format:** Full multi-page or single-page PDF.
* **Branding:** College letterhead, official logo, contact details, and physical address.
* **Required Content:**
  * Congratulations message.
  * Admission details (Admitted Program, Intake Period, Reporting/Admission Date).
  * Reporting procedures and registration steps.
  * List of physical documents to present on arrival (Academic certificates, identification, passport photos).
  * Uniform, dress code, or department-specific regulations.
  * Signature and official stamp of the Academic Registrar or Principal.

---

## 2. Fee Structure (`official_fee_structure`)
* **Format:** Full PDF document.
* **Branding:** Official college letterhead.
* **Required Content:**
  * Clear title stating the targeted program or intake year.
  * Itemized cost schedule per term/semester:
    * Tuition fees.
    * Statutory fees (Library, Activity, Exam, Caution Money, Registration, ICT).
    * Boarding/Accommodation fees (if applicable).
  * Bank payment instructions (Bank Name, Branch, Account Name, Account Number).
  * Mobile money instructions (M-Pesa Paybill number and Account format).
  * Financial terms (e.g., late payment policies, instalment policies, refund terms).

---

## 3. Payment Receipt (`official_payment_receipt`)
* **Format:** Background PDF template or high-resolution image (`.png` / `.jpg`).
* **Branding:** Institutional letterhead frame, logo watermark in the center.
* **Required Content Layout (Keep Center Clear):**
  * Top header with college branding.
  * Signature lines and official stamp placeholders at the bottom.
  * **System Overlaid Data (Leave Blank space in the center):**
    * Receipt Number
    * Payment Date
    * Student Name & Admission Number
    * Amount Paid (in figures & words)
    * Payment Mode (M-Pesa, Cash, Bank Deposit)
    * Outstanding Balance

---

## 4. Exam Pass (`official_exam_pass`)
* **Format:** Background PDF template or high-resolution card frame.
* **Branding:** Standard header branding, watermark background.
* **Required Content Layout:**
  * Section detailing exam regulations (e.g., "Cheating leads to automatic suspension", "Must carry valid student ID").
  * **System Overlaid Data (Leave Blank space in the center/grid):**
    * Student Name & Admission Number
    * Student Passport Photo frame
    * Registered Units list for the current exam session
    * Invigilator/Supervisor sign-off column
    * Registrar's stamp

---

## 5. Internship Letter (`official_internship_letter`)
* **Format:** Standard single-page letter PDF.
* **Branding:** College letterhead, department logo.
* **Required Content:**
  * Standard reference header: `TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN`.
  * Core body text introducing the student, verifying their enrollment status, program of study, and requesting placement for mandatory industrial attachment.
  * Signature block of the Dean of Students, Principal, or Attachment Coordinator.
  * Institutional contact details (Phone/Email) for attachment host verification.

---

## 6. Certificate Template (`official_certificate_template`)
* **Format:** High-resolution border frame image (`.png`) or PDF template.
* **Branding:** High-quality decorative borders, college crest, background security guilloche pattern.
* **Required Content Layout:**
  * Title block: `Risabu Technical College` (or similar).
  * Classical text: `This is to certify that...`
  * Date of Award and Certificate Serial Number placeholder.
  * Signatures of the Principal and the Chairman of the Board.
  * **System Overlaid Data (Leave Center Blank):**
    * Graduate's Name
    * Certificate/Diploma Title
    * Classification / Grade (e.g., Distinction, Credit)
